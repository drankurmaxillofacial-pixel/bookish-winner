What's the Best Age for Braces?

Written by Dr. Ankur Rathaur, BDS, MDS (Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery) — 6+ years of clinical experience

Parents in Kanpur often ask the same question: "Should we wait, or start braces now?" The honest answer is that there isn't one single best age — there's a best age for a first checkup, and a different best age for full treatment. Here's how to tell which stage your child (or you) is at.

Age 7: The First Orthodontic Checkup

The Indian Orthodontic Society and international guidelines both recommend a first orthodontic evaluation around age 7, once the first permanent molars and front teeth have come in. At this stage braces aren't fitted — but this checkup catches problems early, such as:

  • Crossbite or underbite affecting jaw growth
  • Severe crowding or missing teeth
  • Thumb-sucking or mouth-breathing habits affecting bite development

Catching these early sometimes means simpler, shorter treatment later — or occasionally avoiding full braces altogether.

Age 10-14: The Ideal Window for Full Braces

Most children get braces during this window because by then most or all baby teeth have been replaced by permanent teeth, and the jaw is still growing — which makes it easier to guide teeth and jaw alignment together. This is why you'll see so many students in this age group wearing braces; it's genuinely the most efficient stage to treat.

Benefits of treating in this window:

  1. Jaw bone is more responsive to gentle pressure, so movement is faster.
  2. Growth can be guided, sometimes correcting bite issues without surgery later.
  3. Treatment is often shorter compared to starting as an adult.

Teenagers (15-19)

Braces still work very well in the teenage years, though jaw growth has mostly slowed by the late teens. Teens are usually excellent candidates for either metal braces, ceramic (tooth-colored) braces, or clear aligners, depending on budget and how visible they want the treatment to be.

Is It Too Late for Adult Braces?

No. There is no upper age limit for orthodontic treatment as long as the gums and supporting bone are healthy — and we treat working professionals in their 30s and 40s regularly at Dental Home. The main differences for adults:

  • Treatment can take slightly longer since jaw growth has stopped.
  • Gum health needs to be checked first — adults with gum disease need that treated before braces.
  • Discreet options like ceramic braces or clear aligners are popular for professionals who want a less visible treatment.

Signs Your Child (or You) May Need Braces

  • Crowded, overlapping, or gapped teeth
  • Difficulty biting or chewing comfortably
  • Jaw that shifts or clicks when opening/closing
  • Teeth that don't meet properly when the mouth is closed (overbite/underbite/crossbite)
  • Speech affected by tooth position

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best age for a child to get braces?

Most orthodontic treatment with full braces begins between ages 10 and 14, once most permanent teeth have erupted. A first checkup around age 7 is still recommended to catch issues early.

Is it too late to get braces as an adult?

No. There is no upper age limit for braces as long as gums and bone are healthy. Many adults successfully complete treatment with clear aligners or ceramic braces.

How long do braces take to work?

Average treatment time is 12 to 24 months depending on case complexity, patient age, and adherence to instructions.
