Root Canal Cost in Kanpur: 2026 Price Breakdown

Written by Dr. Ankur Rathaur, BDS, MDS (Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery) — 6+ years of clinical experience

If you've been told you need a root canal treatment (RCT) and you're trying to figure out what it will actually cost in Kanpur, this guide breaks it down honestly — no vague "starting from" numbers, no hidden crown charges.

Average Root Canal Cost in Kanpur

Root canal pricing in Kanpur varies mainly by which tooth is treated and how many canals it has. Front teeth (incisors and canines) have a single canal and are the least expensive. Back teeth (premolars and molars) have 2-4 canals and take longer chair time, so they cost more.

Tooth Type Canals Typical Cost (RCT only)
Front tooth (incisor/canine)1₹2,500 – ₹4,000
Premolar1-2₹3,000 – ₹5,500
Molar2-4₹4,000 – ₹8,000
Re-treatment (failed old RCT)Varies₹5,000 – ₹10,000

Note: these figures cover the root canal procedure only. The tooth still needs a crown afterward — see below.

Don't Forget the Crown Cost

After a root canal, especially on a molar, the tooth is weaker and needs a crown (cap) to protect it from cracking. This is billed separately and is often the part patients aren't warned about:

  • Metal crown: ₹1,500 – ₹3,000 — strong, budget-friendly, less cosmetic.
  • PFM (porcelain-fused-to-metal) crown: ₹3,500 – ₹6,000 — good balance of strength and appearance.
  • Zirconia / ceramic crown: ₹8,000 – ₹15,000 — best aesthetics, ideal for front teeth.

What Affects Your Final Bill

  1. Number of canals — more canals means more filling material and more chair time.
  2. Infection severity — an abscessed tooth may need an extra sitting to drain infection before the canal is sealed.
  3. Re-treatment vs. first-time RCT — removing old filling material is harder and takes longer.
  4. Crown material — this is usually the single biggest swing factor in your total bill.
  5. Rotary vs. hand instrumentation — rotary (motorized) files clean canals more thoroughly and are used as standard at Dental Home.

Is a Cheap Root Canal Worth the Risk?

A root canal that isn't properly cleaned and sealed can fail months or years later — and re-treatment costs more than doing it right the first time. At Dental Home, every RCT is performed under Dr. Ankur Rathaur's supervision using rotary instrumentation and is X-ray verified before the final filling, so you're not paying twice for the same tooth.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a root canal cost in Kanpur?

A single root canal treatment (RCT) in Kanpur typically costs between ₹2,500 and ₹5,000 per tooth for a front tooth, and ₹4,000 to ₹8,000 for a molar, before the crown.

Does root canal cost include the crown?

No. The root canal procedure and the crown are billed separately. A crown can add anywhere from ₹1,500 for a metal crown to ₹8,000–₹15,000 for a ceramic or zirconia crown.

Is root canal painful?

Root canal treatment is performed under local anesthesia, so the procedure itself is not painful. Most patients feel mild sensitivity for 1-2 days afterward.
